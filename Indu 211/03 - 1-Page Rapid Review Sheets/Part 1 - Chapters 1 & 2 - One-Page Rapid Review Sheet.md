## Part 1: Chapters 1 & 2 — Foundations of Industrial & Systems Engineering

---

### 1. Etymology & Foundational Duality
* **Root**: Latin ***ingenium*** = inborn talent, cleverness, creative inventiveness $\to$ Engineer = inventive problem-solver.
* **Science vs. Engineering**:
  * **Science**: Quest for *basic knowledge* | Method: Observe $\to$ Hypothesize $\to$ Controlled Experiment | Output: Theories.
  * **Engineering**: Application of science to solve human problems | Goal: *A better life* | Output: Functional systems.
  * **Interplay**: Science feeds discoveries to engineering; engineering feeds problems back to science. Math is the common foundation.

---

### 2. Chronology of Pioneers (Industrial Revolution $\to$ Scientific Management)
* **Charles Babbage** (1800s): **Division of Labor**; specialized tasks reduce wage/training costs; harmonious labor relations.
* **Frederick W. Taylor** (1900s): **"Scientific Management"**; stopwatch time studies; standardization of tools & work methods.
* **Henry Ford** (1913): **Mass Production & Moving Assembly Line**; interchangeable parts; simple jobs for minimally trained workers.
* **Frank & Lillian Gilbreth** (1910s): **Motion Analysis & Human Factors**; eliminated wasted motions (therbligs); ergonomics, fatigue & psychology.
* **Henry L. Gantt** (1917): **Gantt Chart**; visual milestone bar chart comparing planned vs. actual project schedules.
* **Walter Shewhart** (1920s): **Statistical Quality Control (SQC)**; control charts separating random noise from assignable defects.
* **Operations Research (WWII)**: Applying mathematical modeling, optimization & linear programming to operational logistics $\to$ Big Data & AI.

---

### 3. Professional Regulation & Ethics (Quebec & Canada)
* **Regulatory Hierarchy**: **Engineers Canada** (National body) $\to$ **CEAB** (Accredits B.Eng degrees) $\to$ **OIQ** (Quebec provincial regulator, ~55k P.Engs).
* **Legal Definition ("Practice of Engineering")**: Any act of planning, designing, composing, evaluating, advising, reporting, directing, supervising, or managing work requiring engineering principles and concerning the safeguarding of **life, health, property, economic interests, public welfare, or the environment**.
* **Ethical Mandate**: When company cost pressures conflict with public safety, the engineer's primary legal & ethical obligation is **ALWAYS to public safety**.

---

### 4. Systems Theory & The Production Model
* **System**: A set of interrelated components acting together toward a common objective.
* **Open-Loop**: No feedback, cannot self-correct (*"car without a driver"*).
* **Closed-Loop**: Monitors output, compares against standard, applies feedback control (*"car with a driver"*).
* **Universal Production Loop**:
  $$\text{Inputs (Capital, Materials, Labor, Market)} \longrightarrow \mathbf{[\text{Conversion Process}]} \longrightarrow \text{Outputs (Goods/Services)}$$
  $$\uparrow\text{---------------------- Corrective Action } \longleftarrow \text{ Performance Monitoring / Feedback ----------------------}\downarrow$$

### 5. The Decision-Making Hierarchy
| Level | Time Horizon | Key Questions | Typical Industrial Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Strategic** | Long-term ($3-10\text{ yrs}$) | **What, How, Where?** | New product lines, plant locations, capital investments. |
| **Tactical** | Medium-term ($1-12\text{ mos}$) | **How much, When?** | Aggregate production schedules, inventory safety stock, seasonal hiring. |
| **Control** | Short-term (Daily/Hourly) | **Keep line running!** | Shift worker dispatching, machine repair, tracking scrap/defects. |

### 6. The IE Identity & Practice
* **Core Motto**: Work with **PEOPLE** to make systems **BETTER, FASTER, SAFER, CHEAPER**.
* **Two Design Levels**:
  1. **Human Activity Systems**: Physical workplace (layout, ergonomics, methods, safety, material handling).
  2. **Management Control Systems**: Policies & metrics (forecasting, MRP, inventory, quality control, scheduling).
* **Human Factor (Dale Carnegie)**: $\mathbf{15\%}$ financial success is technical knowledge; $\mathbf{85\%}$ is human engineering (leadership, communication, empathy).
* **IE in the Age of AI**: Enhances IE; enables predictive maintenance, real-time bottleneck detection, and digital twin simulation.
