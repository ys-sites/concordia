# INDU 211 · Visual Learning AI Videos

One narrated "visual learning" video per math topic of INDU 211 (Chapters 3–8, 14, 15 and 17), drawn in a clean hand-drawn doodle style. Each video builds the idea, shows how the topic appears on the exam, teaches the method step by step, then solves several problems of the same kind with every number worked out (the teacher's lecture examples first), and ends with the traps and a recap. On the website each video sits in the matching Learn lesson. The MP4s are served from the media CDN.
