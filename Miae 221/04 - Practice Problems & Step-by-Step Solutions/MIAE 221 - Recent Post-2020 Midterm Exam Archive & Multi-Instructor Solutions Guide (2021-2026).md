### Detailed Analysis of Recent Post-2020 Midterm Papers

#### 1. 2025 Midterm Examination — Version A
![Exam Header 1 — 2025 Midterm Version A](./images/exam_header_1.png)

- **Official Header**: `MIAE 221 – Materials Science – Midterm 2025, Version A`
- **Source Reference**: Studocu Document ID `124560047`
- **Key Concepts Analyzed**:
  1. **Interatomic Force vs. Energy Curves**:
     - Equilibrium spacing $r_0$ occurs where net force $F_N = 0$, but where net potential energy $E_N$ is at its absolute minimum trough ($\frac{dE}{dr} = 0$).
     - Deep, steep potential wells correspond to high melting temperature ($T_m$), high elastic modulus ($E$), and low coefficient of thermal expansion ($\alpha$).
  2. **FCC & BCC Geometry**:
     - FCC close-packed directions: $\langle 110 \rangle$, close-packed planes: $\{111\}$.
     - BCC close-packed directions: $\langle 111 \rangle$, close-packed planes: $\{110\}$.
  3. **Bragg's Law and Selection Rules**:
     - BCC reflects only planes where $h + k + l = \text{even}$ (first reflection: $(110)$, second: $(200)$).
     - FCC reflects only planes where $h, k, l$ are all unmixed (all even or all odd: $(111)$, $(200)$, $(220)$, $(311)$).

#### 2. Winter 2026 Midterm Examination — Drs. Mamadou Sy & Navid Sharifi
![Exam Header 6 — Winter 2026 Midterm](./images/exam_header_6.png)

- **Official Header**: `MIAE 221 – Midterm, Winter 2026; Instructors: Drs. Mamadou Sy & Navid Sharifi`
- **Source Reference**: Studocu Document ID `159540505`
- **Key Concepts Analyzed**:
  1. **Non-Steady-State Diffusion (Fick's Second Law)**:
     $$\frac{C_x - C_0}{C_s - C_0} = 1 - \text{erf}\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$
     - When determining case depth or carbon penetration time, $\frac{x^2}{Dt} = \text{constant}$.
     - Doubling the diffusion depth requires increasing the heat-treatment time by a factor of $4$ ($t_2 = 4t_1$).
  2. **Linear and Planar Density Calculations**:
     - Planar Density ($PD$) on $(110)$ plane in BCC iron:
       $$PD_{(110)} = \frac{\text{Number of equivalent atoms centered on plane}}{\text{Area of plane}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{2} a^2} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{a^2}$$
     - Linear Density ($LD$) along $[110]$ direction in FCC copper:
       $$LD_{[110]} = \frac{2 \text{ atoms}}{\sqrt{2}a} = \frac{2}{4R} = \frac{1}{2R}$$

#### 3. Fall 2025 Midterm Examination — Section X (Dr. Sam Eskandarian)
![Exam Header 7 — Fall 2025 Midterm Version A](./images/exam_header_7.png)

- **Official Header**: `MIAE 221/X – Midterm, Fall 2025; Instructor: Dr. Sam Eskandarian`
- **Source Reference**: Studocu Document ID `144474318`
- **Key Concepts Analyzed**:
  1. **Schmid's Law & Critical Resolved Shear Stress (CRSS)**:
     $$\tau_R = \sigma \cos \phi \cos \lambda$$
     - Slip initiates when the resolved shear stress reaches the critical value: $\tau_R \ge \tau_{\text{CRSS}}$.
     - The maximum resolved shear stress occurs when $\phi = \lambda = 45^\circ$, giving $\tau_R = \frac{\sigma}{2}$ (Schmid factor $m = 0.5$).
     - If slip direction or slip plane normal is perpendicular to the tensile axis ($\phi = 90^\circ$ or $\lambda = 90^\circ$), $\tau_R = 0$ and no slip occurs.
  2. **Engineering vs. True Stress/Strain**:
     $$\sigma_T = \sigma_E(1 + \epsilon_E) \quad \text{and} \quad \epsilon_T = \ln(1 + \epsilon_E)$$
     *(Valid up to the onset of necking at maximum load / UTS).*

#### 4. Fall 2024 Midterm Examination — Sections T & Y (Drs. Martin Pugh & Navid Sharifi)
![Exam Header 5 — Fall 2024 Sections T & Y](./images/exam_header_5.png)

- **Official Header**: `MIAE 221 – T and Y – Midterm Solutions Fall 2024; Instructors: Drs. Martin Pugh & Navid Sharifi`
- **Source Reference**: Studocu Document ID `108649883`
- **Key Concepts Analyzed**:
  1. **Point Defects & Arrhenius Vacancy Concentrations**:
     $$N_v = N \exp\left(-\frac{Q_v}{k T}\right)$$
     - $N = \frac{\rho N_A}{A}$ (atomic sites per unit volume).
     - Temperature $T$ must always be converted to Kelvin ($K = ^\circ\text{C} + 273.15$).
     - Boltzmann constant $k = 8.617 \times 10^{-5}\text{ eV/K}$ or $1.38 \times 10^{-23}\text{ J/K}$.
  2. **Hume-Rothery Rules for Complete Solid Solubility**:
     - $\Delta R \le 15\%$ (atomic size factor).
     - Crystal structures must be identical (e.g. both FCC for Cu and Ni).
     - Electronegativities must be similar.
     - Valences must be identical or higher valency metal preferred as solute.

#### 5. Fall 2024 Midterm Examination — Section X-A (Dr. Martin Pugh)
![Exam Header 2 — Fall 2024 Section X-A](./images/exam_header_2.png)

- **Official Header**: `MIAE 221 – Materials Science – Midterm Fall 2024, Section X-A; Instructor: Dr. Martin Pugh`
- **Source Reference**: Studocu Document ID `112451179` & `110496507`
- **Key Concepts Analyzed**:
  1. **Diffusion Mechanisms**:
     - Vacancy (substitutional) diffusion requires both vacancy creation and atomic jumps ($Q_d = Q_v + Q_m$).
     - Interstitial diffusion ($C, H, N, O$ in iron) occurs much faster with lower activation energy because interstitial sites are largely empty.
  2. **Stress-Strain Landmarks**:
     - Proportional Limit: end of strictly linear elastic behavior.
     - 0.2% Offset Yield Strength: draw line parallel to modulus slope at $\epsilon = 0.002$.
     - Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS): maximum engineering stress prior to necking.
     - Fracture Stress: stress at complete separation (lower than UTS in engineering stress, but highest in true stress).

### Core Formula Reference & Exam Quick-Check

1. **Lattice Dimensions & Atomic Packing**:
   - FCC: $a = 2\sqrt{2} R$, Coordination Number $= 12$, Atoms/cell $n = 4$, $\text{APF} = 0.74$.
   - BCC: $a = \frac{4}{\sqrt{3}} R$, Coordination Number $= 8$, Atoms/cell $n = 2$, $\text{APF} = 0.68$.
   - HCP: $c/a = 1.633$, Coordination Number $= 12$, Atoms/cell $n = 6$, $\text{APF} = 0.74$.

2. **Interplanar Spacing (Cubic)**:
   $$d_{hkl} = \frac{a}{\sqrt{h^2 + k^2 + l^2}}$$

3. **Bragg's Diffraction Law**:
   $$\lambda = 2 d_{hkl} \sin \theta$$

4. **Temperature-Dependent Diffusion Coefficient**:
   $$D = D_0 \exp\left(-\frac{Q_d}{R T}\right)$$
