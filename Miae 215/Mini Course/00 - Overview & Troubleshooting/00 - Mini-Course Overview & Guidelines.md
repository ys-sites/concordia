## 1. Course Purpose & The 2-Week Head Start

This online mini-course is designed to get engineering students programming with C++ in a short period of time (2 weeks). This "head start" provides students with a solid background to learn more advanced C++ topics covered in **MIAE 215** (also known as **MECH 215**) and hit the ground running in a busy first term.

A large number of students fall behind in MIAE 215 due to an insufficient computer and programming background. The small amount of time you invest (about **5 to 10 hours total**) with this mini-course could save you dozens of hours of struggle later in the semester.

> **Important Academic Notice:**  
> There are **no labs or tutorials during the first three weeks of the term**. Instead, students are expected to complete Lessons #1 through #4 during the first week, and all remaining lessons (#5 and #6) by the end of the second week. Regular MIAE 215 lectures strictly assume you have completed this mini-course!

## 2. Why Mechanical, Aerospace & Industrial Engineers Need C++

C++ programming is a vital core skill for modern engineers:
* **The Language of Automation**: Computers execute industrial automation, CNC machining, robotic assembly lines, and flight control systems.
* **Mechatronics & Embedded Systems**: Departmental project courses such as **MECH 390** (mini-capstone involving mobile robots) and **MECH 490** (capstone mechatronics projects) heavily rely on Arduino and ARM microcontroller boards programmed in C++.
* **Competitive Clubs & Society Teams**: Engineering teams including **Space Concordia** (rocketry, satellites, rovers) and **SAE Concordia** (Formula racing, aero design) write high-performance telemetry, flight software, and sensor acquisition loops in C++.
