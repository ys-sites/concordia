Atom positions, radii, cell edges and labels remain generated from the existing
crystallographic model. No external model is used: the exact geometry is the
lesson. Consequently GLB loading and Draco compression do not apply to this proof.

The spheres represent a polished metal teaching apparatus, not a literal
photograph of atoms. Role colors are educational coding, not chemical identity.
Smooth polished surfaces use uniform metalness/roughness and the HDR reflection
map; no scanned surface normal, albedo or roughness maps are included. The matte
studio floor is a neutral dielectric. Other suggested asset libraries remain
candidates for a later, separately approved rollout; none is bundled here.
