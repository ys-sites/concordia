1. Bonding, Potential Energy Wells & Properties

At equilibrium r₀: F_N = F_A + F_R = 0, E_N reaches minimum (-E₀)
  • Melting Temp (T_m): Proportional to well depth E₀ (deeper well → higher T_m).
  • Modulus of Elasticity (E): Proportional to curvature at trough: E ∝ (d²E_N/dr²) at r₀. Steeper slope = stiffer material.
  • Thermal Expansion (α_L): Governed by well asymmetry. Symmetric well (deep) = low α_L (ceramics). Asymmetric = high α_L (polymers).
  • Bond Types: Ionic (nondirectional, electron transfer); Covalent (directional, sharing); Metallic (delocalized sea of e⁻, ductile slip); Hydrogen (strongest secondary, ~50 kJ/mol, polymers).

3. Crystallographic Directions & Planes

  • Directions [uvw]: Vector tail at origin (translate if negative). Head coordinates in a,b,c. Clear fractions to integers. Family: ⟨uvw⟩.
  • Planes (hkl): MUST NOT pass through origin! (Shift origin if needed). Read intercepts A,B,C. Take reciprocals (1/A, 1/B, 1/C; 1/∞=0). Clear fractions. Family: {hkl}.
  • Hexagonal (hkil): 4-axis Miller-Bravais. Always satisfies i = -(h + k).
  • Linear Density: LD = (atoms centered on line) / (length L).
  • Planar Density: PD = (2D atom circles in plane) / (area A).

4. XRD & Bragg's Law Selection Rules

Bragg's Law: nλ = 2 d_hkl sinθ   |   Cubic: d_hkl = a / √(h² + k² + l²)
  • BCC Rule: Peaks occur ONLY when h+k+l is EVEN: (110), (200), (211), (220). (100) and (111) are forbidden!
  • FCC Rule: Peaks occur ONLY when h,k,l are ALL ODD or ALL EVEN: (111), (200), (220), (311). (110) is forbidden!

5. Imperfections & Hume-Rothery Rules

Vacancy Equilibrium: N_v = N exp(-Q_v / kT)   [k = 8.62×10⁻⁵ eV/K]
  • Hume-Rothery (Complete Solid Solubility, e.g. Cu-Ni):
    1. Size: |Δr| / r_solvent < 15% · 2. Crystal structure: identical (both FCC)
    3. Electronegativity: comparable · 4. Valency: higher valency preferred.

6. Dislocations, Slip & Schmid's Law

  • Edge: Extra half-plane; Burgers vector b ⊥ dislocation line.
  • Screw: Spiral ramp; Burgers vector b ∥ dislocation line.
  • FCC Slip: {111}⟨110⟩ → 4 × 3 = 12 systems (high ductility).
  • BCC Slip: {110}⟨111⟩ → 6 × 2 = 12 systems (total 48; high strength).
Schmid's Law: τ_R = σ cosφ cosλ   |   Max slip at φ = λ = 45°: σ_y = 2 τ_CRSS
  • Strengthening: 1. Hall-Petch: σ_y = σ₀ + k_y d⁻¹/² (finer grains = stronger) · 2. Solid solution pinning · 3. Cold work (%CW = (A₀ - A_d)/A₀ × 100%; increases σ_y and UTS, lowers %EL).

7. Diffusion in Solids (Fick's Laws)

  • Mechanisms: Interstitial (C in Fe) is much faster than Vacancy. Cu-Ni couple undergoes interdiffusion (both diffuse simultaneously).
  • Fick's 1st (Steady-State): J = -D (dC/dx) = -D (C_A - C_B)/(x_A - x_B).
  • Arrhenius: D = D₀ exp(-Q_d / RT) → ln D vs 1/T slope = -Q_d/R.
  • Fick's 2nd (Non-Steady): (C(x,t) - C₀)/(C_s - C₀) = 1 - erf(x / 2√(Dt)).
  • Scaling: Constant composition → x² / (D · t) = const.

8. Mechanical Properties & Stress-Strain

σ = F/A₀ [MPa]  |  ε = Δl/l₀  |  σ = Eε (Hooke)  |  ν = -ε_x/ε_z ≈ 0.33
  • Elastic Modulus E: Dictated by bond stiffness ONLY. Cold work, grain size, and alloying do NOT change E! (All steels E ≈ 207 GPa).
  • Point B: Proportional limit. Point C: 0.2% offset yield (σ_y).
  • Point E (UTS): Maximum engineering stress. Necking begins strictly AFTER Point E!
  • Uniform plastic: Point C to E. Non-uniform (necking): Point E to fracture.
  • True Stress/Strain (up to necking): σ_T = σ(1 + ε), ε_T = ln(1 + ε).
  • Resilience: U_r ≈ σ_y² / (2E). Toughness: Total area under σ-ε curve.
