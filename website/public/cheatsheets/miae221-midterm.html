1. Bonding, Potential Wells & Properties
Equilibrium r₀: F_N = F_A + F_R = 0; E_N minimum (-E₀)
  • Melting T_m: Deeper trough E₀ → higher bond energy → higher T_m.
  • Modulus E: Curvature at trough: E ∝ (d²E_N/dr²) at r₀. Steeper = stiffer.
  • Expansion α_L: Governed by well asymmetry. Symmetric well (deep) = low α_L (ceramics). Asymmetric = high α_L (polymers).
  • Bonds: Ionic (electron transfer, nondirectional); Covalent (electron sharing, directional); Metallic (delocalized sea of e⁻, ductile slip); Hydrogen (strongest secondary, ~50 kJ/mol).

3. Miller Indices, Directions & Planes
  • Directions [uvw]: Tail at origin; head in a,b,c; clear fractions; overbar for negative. Family: ⟨uvw⟩.
  • Planes (hkl): NEVER pass through origin! (Shift origin if needed). Read intercepts A,B,C; take reciprocals (1/A, 1/B, 1/C; 1/∞=0); clear fractions. Family: {hkl}.
  • Hexagonal (hkil): 4 axes (a1, a2, a3 at 120°, z). Always: i = -(h + k).
  • Densities: LD = atoms on line / length L; PD = 2D atoms in plane / area A.
4. XRD & Bragg's Law Selection Rules
Bragg's Law: nλ = 2 d_hkl sinθ  |  Cubic: d_hkl = a / √(h² + k² + l²)
  • BCC Peak Rule: h + k + l must be EVEN: (110), (200), (211), (220). (100) and (111) are forbidden!
  • FCC Peak Rule: h, k, l must be ALL ODD or ALL EVEN: (111), (200), (220), (311). (110) and (211) are forbidden!
5. Defects & Hume-Rothery Rules
Vacancies: N_v = N exp(-Q_v / kT)   [k = 8.62×10⁻⁵ eV/K]
  • Hume-Rothery (Complete Solid Solubility, Cu-Ni):
    1. Size: |Δr|/r_solvent < 15% · 2. Crystal: identical (both FCC)
    3. Electronegativity: comparable · 4. Valency: higher dissolves more.
6. Slip Systems & Schmid's Law
  • Edge: Extra half-plane; Burgers b ⊥ line. Screw: Spiral ramp; b ∥ line.
  • FCC Slip: {111}⟨110⟩ → 4×3 = 12 systems (high ductility).
  • BCC Slip: {110}⟨111⟩ → 6×2 = 12 systems (total 48; high strength).
Schmid: τ_R = σ cosφ cosλ  |  Max slip at φ=λ=45°: σ_y = 2 τ_CRSS
  • Strengthening: 1. Hall-Petch: σ_y = σ₀ + k_y d⁻¹/² (finer grains = stronger) · 2. Solid solution pinning · 3. Cold work: %CW = (A₀ - A_d)/A₀ × 100% (increases σ_y and UTS, reduces %EL).
7. Diffusion (Fick's Laws)
  • Mechanisms: Interstitial (C in Fe) is much faster than Vacancy. Cu-Ni couple undergoes interdiffusion (both diffuse simultaneously).
  • Fick 1st (Steady): J = -D (dC/dx) = -D (C_A - C_B)/(x_A - x_B).
  • Arrhenius: D = D₀ exp(-Q_d / RT) → ln D vs 1/T slope = -Q_d/R.
  • Fick 2nd (Semi-Infinite): (C(x,t) - C₀)/(C_s - C₀) = 1 - erf(x / 2√(Dt)).
  • Scaling: Constant composition → x² / (D · t) = constant.
8. Mechanical Properties & Stress-Strain
σ = F/A₀ [MPa]  |  ε = Δl/l₀  |  σ = Eε (Hooke)  |  ν = -ε_x/ε_z ≈ 0.33
  • Elastic Modulus E: Depends on bond stiffness ONLY. Cold work, grain size, and alloying do NOT change E! (Steels E ≈ 207 GPa).
  • Point B: Proportional limit. Point C: 0.2% offset yield (σ_y).
  • Point E (UTS): Max stress. Necking begins strictly AFTER Point E!
  • Uniform plastic: Point C to E. Non-uniform (necking): Point E to fracture.
  • True Stress/Strain (up to necking): σ_T = σ(1 + ε), ε_T = ln(1 + ε).
  • Resilience: U_r ≈ σ_y² / (2E). Toughness: Total area under σ-ε curve.
